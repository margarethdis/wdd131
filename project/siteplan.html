Camino a Políglota

01

Site Name

Camino a Políglota ("Path to Polyglot")

I chose this name because it describes exactly what the project is: a journey, not a finish line. "Camino" (path) conveys that language learning is an ongoing process with progress and setbacks, and "políglota" (polyglot) makes clear where that path is headed: fluency in several languages (English, French, Japanese, and Italian). The name also works as a promise to the Spanish-speaking audience that already follows me on social media: here they'll find the behind-the-scenes of that process.

Optional domain availability: caminoapoliglota.com

02

Site Purpose

The site documents, for a Spanish-speaking audience interested in languages, my personal process of learning English, French, Japanese, and Italian at the same time. It works as a blog with articles reflecting on my study methods and real progress (including mistakes), plus a contact page so other language learners can reach out. In the medium term, the site aims to grow into a community and, eventually, into a source of income tied to this content.
